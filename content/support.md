Write to {{<email />}}.

It helps to say:

- the build number, from Settings → About Legium
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened
- for a page that was read wrongly, the photograph, if you are willing to send it

Nothing in your library reaches us unless you send it. See [Privacy](@/privacy.md).

## Before you write

**Keep a copy of your library.** Settings → Export everything… writes the whole library to
one file, and Import an archive… reads it back.

**Something you deleted.** Deleted passages wait in Settings → Trash, where you can restore
them.

**What Legium needs.** An iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or later.
